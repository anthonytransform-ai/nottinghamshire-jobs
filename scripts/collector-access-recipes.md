# Source access recipes and expansion assessments

ANT-259 mechanical traversal knowledge and ANT-260 expansion assessments,
established 12 October 2026. These recipes reference registry IDs.
`source-registry.json` remains the sole owner of source routes, families,
methods and allowed fallbacks. Eligibility, geography, classification,
deduplication and participant summaries remain Playbook-owned.

Counts and control states below are dated observations from the 2026-10-12 proof,
not expected counts for future runs. Every future run must dynamically enumerate
the current live source and apply the completion/failure contracts below.

## university-of-nottingham

- **Registry ID:** `university-of-nottingham`.
- **List:** request the exact registered all-vacancies route using ordinary HTTP.
  The response contains `.vacancylist` sections and individual vacancy paragraphs.
  Do not reconstruct the list from categories. Retain section headings as raw
  context, including China, studentship and internal-only material for judgement.
- **Identity/link:** inside each section, retain each actual anchor whose path
  ends in `vacancy.aspx` and whose query exposes `ref`. Resolve its observed href
  against the response URL. The `ref` is the public reference; do not invent IDs.
- **Advert:** ordinary HTTP GET of the retained individual href.
- **Completion:** process the full response, and follow any exposed normal Next
  control. A repeated/ambiguous Next route is a failure. In the 2026-10-12 proof,
  the route was one full page with no Next control. Zero requires a recognised
  list surface and explicit no-vacancies evidence, not merely missing anchors.
- **Details:** `h1.pagetitle`, `.advertheader` labelled paragraphs (Location,
  Salary, Closing Date, Reference), and the `#main` body. Preserve raw wording;
  contract/hours embedded in prose remain available for agent interpretation.
- **OK/EMPTY:** normal list completion plus completed readable detail processing
  for every captured vacancy; EMPTY additionally requires explicit genuine zero.
- **PARTIAL/ERROR:** useful list candidates plus interrupted pagination/detail
  failures -> PARTIAL. Inaccessible/unrecognised/challenge list with no trustworthy
  candidates -> ERROR. Neither can become EMPTY.
- **Fallback:** only the registered instructions; exact all-vacancies start and
  observed individual hrefs. No category reconstruction or broad discovery.
- **Completed proof — 2026-10-12:** local `diagnosis/university-of-nottingham.html`,
  `diagnosis/uon-detail.html`, and `pilot-2026-10-12` cached pages/checkpoint/raw
  evidence. The collector completed with OK: 69 live identities and 69 readable
  details. The separate normal-route identity comparison matched all 69 identities
  with no missing or extra items. Future vacancy counts are dynamic.

## nottingham-trent-university

- **Registry ID:** `nottingham-trent-university`.
- **List:** render the registered jobs surface in ordinary Chromium/Edge. Wait
  for `#totalMatchRecords` plus actual `.job-card` advert links (or verified zero).
  In the 2026-10-12 proof, the initial rendered response exposed 12 cards out of
  35 matching jobs.
- **Identity/link:** retain the actual card `a[href*="JobDetail"]` href and its
  `JobId` query value. Keep the separately labelled `Job reference` as the public
  reference; platform ID and public reference are different.
- **Advert:** GET the retained href. The normal detail HTTP response exposes one
  `application/ld+json` JobPosting with useful description, employmentType and
  validThrough. Preserve labelled list location, pay and employment facts.
- **Load More:** click the exposed `#searchResultsLoadMoreBtn`, wait for unique
  advert href count to grow, then capture the rendered cards. The portal appends
  duplicate `#totalMatchRecords` elements after Load More: read all, require
  agreeing numeric values, and fail on conflict. Do not assume a unique ID node.
  Finish only when Load More is absent/hidden AND unique identities equal the
  exposed agreeing result count. Exhausted/stalled load or count mismatch fails.
  Dismiss the initial optional-cookie banner before waiting for the initial
  cards, because dismissal can rerender the result surface. Do not force clicks
  through the portal's loading overlay.
- **Details:** JobPosting description HTML converted to readable raw text;
  labelled card facts and source-exposed references retained separately. No
  classification or participant summary is generated by the adapter.
- **OK/EMPTY:** completed normal Load More sequence and all readable individual
  details. Zero requires the rendered agreeing count of zero plus no job cards;
  static shell zero is insufficient.
- **PARTIAL/ERROR:** interrupted/stalled load or unresolved count/individual detail
  failure with useful candidates -> PARTIAL; no trustworthy rendered evidence ->
  ERROR. The first targeted run correctly ended PARTIAL at 24/35 due to the
  duplicate count nodes and retained all 24 detail pages for reuse.
- **Fallback:** the registry permits rendered current jobs and its normal Load
  More/page path. No Jobtrain internal API/population investigation is needed.
- **Completed proof — 2026-10-12:** local `diagnosis/ntu-rendered.html`,
  `diagnosis/ntu-detail.html`, and the completed pilot's three list snapshots and
  35 readable details. The collector completed with OK. The normal Load More
  sequence was 12 -> 24 -> 35 identities, with counts
  `[35]` -> `[35,35]` -> `[35,35,35]`, and hidden Load More on the final step.
  The separate normal-route identity comparison matched all 35 identities with
  no missing or extra items. Future vacancy counts are dynamic; follow this
  settled recipe without repeating portal discovery.
  Already completed detail work must be reused.

## nottingham-university-hospitals

- **Registry ID:** `nottingham-university-hospitals`.
- **List:** render the registered employer collection in ordinary headed Edge.
  `.hj-job-list` contains an ordered list of `.hj-job` entries. During the
  2026-10-12 proof, diagnosis and in-app browser inspection exposed 45 entries
  with no normal Next control.
- **Identity/link:** each card has its actual `/job/...-v<id>` href. Preserve that
  observed href, the `v<id>` platform identity, and `.hj-jobtitle`,
  `.hj-employername`, `.hj-locationtown`, `.hj-salary` raw facts.
- **Advert:** navigate to each retained href with the same ordinary browser.
  Job summary `.hj-job-details` exposes dt/dd facts: Contract, Hours, Job ref,
  Employer, Site, Town, Salary, Closing. Public Job ref is separate from `v<id>`.
- **Pagination/completion:** follow only an exposed normal Next link, require it
  to remain on the registered employer collection, and detect repeated URLs or
  ambiguous controls. No speculative page numbers or hidden population API.
- **Details:** `#hj-job-advert` and `#hj-job-description` contain overview/duties
  and organisation/person-specification text. Keep expiry/withdrawal notices as
  raw evidence for judgement; geographic labels alone do not establish eligibility.
- **OK/EMPTY:** recognised employer list exhausted normally and every captured
  individual detail processed. Empty requires an explicit no-results message on
  that recognised list, never a security/challenge page.
- **PARTIAL/ERROR:** useful candidates followed by incomplete pagination/details
  -> PARTIAL. HTTP 403, challenge or missing employer list without candidates ->
  ERROR. Source outcome retains method, counts and failures.
- **Fallback:** the registered normal employer collection/individual adverts.
  Ordinary browser rendering is the access mechanism; no challenge solving,
  stealth, proxies or anti-bot bypass. If it becomes blocked, record the limitation.
- **Completed proof — 2026-10-12:** direct HTTP and first headless Edge diagnostic
  returned a security check. Ordinary headed Edge then returned HTTP 200 for the exact
  employer list and an individual advert; the in-app browser independently
  displayed the list. `diagnosis/nuh-rendered.html` and `diagnosis/nuh-detail.html`
  preserve these successful pages. The bounded collector run completed with OK:
  45 live identities and 45 readable details across the full normal path. The
  separate employer-list identity comparison matched all 45 identities with no
  missing or extra items. Future vacancy counts are dynamic; any future failure
  must remain visible through PARTIAL/ERROR.

## govuk-teaching-vacancies

- **Registry ID:** `govuk-teaching-vacancies`.
- **List:** request the exact registered Nottinghamshire query with ordinary
  HTTP. The recognised list surface is `main#main-content` with
  `.search-results__item` entries and a normal `nav[aria-label="Pagination"]`.
  Follow the exposed `rel="next"` link while it remains on the registered
  `teaching-vacancies.service.gov.uk` host. The normal page size observed on
  2026-10-12 was 10.
- **Identity/link:** retain each `.view-vacancy-link` (or equivalent
  `/jobs/<slug>` link) exactly as exposed. The slug is the platform identity;
  do not manufacture a replacement from the title.
- **Advert:** ordinary HTTP GET of the retained `/jobs/<slug>` href. The
  detail page exposes `h1`, the participant-facing address caption, the
  `#job-details` summary list, `.editor-rendered-content` job content and a
  `JobPosting` JSON-LD record. Preserve the optional external application link
  only when the advert exposes it.
- **Completion:** finish every normal `page=` result until there is no `rel=next`
  control. A recognised zero requires the page's explicit zero-result evidence;
  a missing result container is an error. A repeated/ambiguous next route is a
  failure.
- **OK/EMPTY:** normal pagination completion plus readable detail processing for
  every captured vacancy; EMPTY requires an explicit genuine zero.
- **PARTIAL/ERROR:** incomplete pagination or detail failures with candidates ->
  PARTIAL; inaccessible/unrecognised list -> ERROR. Never convert a static or
  blocked zero into EMPTY.
- **Fallback:** use only the registered fixed route and normal `page=` sequence.
  Do not substitute a generic school search or broaden location discovery.
- **Completed proof — 2026-10-12:** the fixed route returned HTTP 200 with 123
  results across 13 visible pages. A live detail page exposed the expected
  slug, Nottinghamshire address, closing date, contract/work pattern, salary,
  job description and optional direct application link. Counts are dated
  observations, not future expectations.

## iTrent / WebRecruitment family

- **Registry IDs:** `ashfield-district-council`, `mansfield-district-council`,
  `bassetlaw-district-council`, `newark-and-sherwood-district-council`,
  `west-nottinghamshire-college`, `north-notts-college-rnn-group`.
- **List:** open the exact registry route and retain its exact WVID. Ordinary
  rendered Chromium/Edge access is the normal method: wait for the registered
  page's `#main-find-jobs`, click the normal `Find jobs` control, then wait for
  `.Mhr-jobSearchMatchCount`/result cards. Do not mix results between WVIDs.
- **Identity/link:** each rendered `.Mhr-jobDetail--listItem` exposes a real
  `vac-id` and `bu-send` detail route. Preserve both. The `VACANCY_ID` in the
  observed route is the platform identity; it is not automatically the human
  `Job reference` shown in the detail facts.
- **Pagination:** process all rendered cards, then click the normal
  `.Mhr-jobSearchMoreResultsButton` while it is visible. Finish only when the
  unique `vac-id` count equals the agreeing exposed result count. A stalled
  load, missing/ambiguous count or early hidden control is PARTIAL, not EMPTY.
- **Advert:** use the observed `bu-send` route in the same rendered session.
  Detail evidence is `.Mhr-jobProfile`, including the title/location/closing
  fields, `Mhr-jobProfile--dataList*` facts, `Mhr-jobProfile--description` and
  the observed `Apply online` href when available.
- **OK/EMPTY:** normal rendered result completion and readable details for every
  captured identity; EMPTY requires the rendered result count to be zero and
  no cards.
- **PARTIAL/ERROR:** detail or load-more failure with useful cards -> PARTIAL;
  blocked/unrecognised rendered board without candidates -> ERROR. A direct
  HTTP 403 or session error is an access limitation, not a zero result.
- **Fallback:** use only each source's registered rendered/current board or
  exact-domain fallback. Do not guess vacancy IDs, inspect undocumented
  population endpoints or merge employers sharing the host.
- **Completed proof — 2026-10-12:** the Ashfield WVID `6777852QI7` rendered
  normally, exposed 12 current jobs, loaded 10 cards followed by two more, and
  exposed a real `VACANCY_ID`/`bu-send` route plus readable detail facts and an
  Apply-online destination. The other five records remain isolated by their
  exact registry WVIDs; counts are dynamic.

## Working-state and investigation boundary

All referenced live pages are ignored under `.weekly-job-update/`, not committed
fixtures or a history database. Use synthetic regression fixtures for the source
shapes and lifecycle edges. Begin a fresh named run for a fresh week's evidence;
resume a same-run checkpoint rather than recrawling completed work. A collector
lock keeps shared source/candidate writes sequential. A terminated process may
leave a lock: check its recorded PID before deliberately removing a stale lock.

These targeted mechanics were verified in the 2026-10-12 proof; future runs follow
the recipes. Changed
markup/access becomes a bounded PARTIAL/ERROR diagnosis, not permission for
repeated speculative exploration. The Skill consumes the raw evidence without
reopening completed list pages. Row finishing and quality audit follow the
current Playbook.
